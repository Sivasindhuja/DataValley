# Cancellation Policy v3

## Rules
- Orders with status PENDING or PROCESSING can be cancelled automatically.
- Orders with status SHIPPED cannot be cancelled automatically; requires manual review via support ticket.
- Orders with status DELIVERED or CANCELLED cannot be cancelled.
- Address updates only allowed before SHIPPED.

## Confirmation
- Cancellation of PENDING orders under $100 does not require confirmation.
- Orders over $100 or PROCESSING require explicit customer confirmation ("yes, cancel").

## Timeline
- Cancellation must be requested within 24 hours for automatic approval if PENDING.
- Manual review tickets are responded to within 48 hours.
