# Shipping Policy v4

- Standard shipping: 3-5 business days domestic, 7-14 international.
- Express shipping: 1-2 business days.
- Carrier updates may lag up to 24 hours.
- Delays beyond 7 business days qualify for support ticket creation.
- Estimated delivery is calculated from shipment date + carrier SLA.
- Customer can track via get_order_status.
